Regulators and laws
	• SEC — Securities and Exchange Commission. Federal regulator; enforces the 1933 and 1934 Acts.
	• FINRA — Financial Industry Regulatory Authority. SRO for broker-dealers and registered reps.
	• SRO — Self-regulatory organization. Writes and enforces member rules under SEC oversight.
	• MSRB — Municipal Securities Rulemaking Board. Writes muni rules; does not enforce them.
	• NASAA — North American Securities Administrators Association. State securities regulators; Series 63/65/66.
	• FRB — Federal Reserve Board. Monetary policy and Regulation T margin.
	• FDIC — Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation. Insures bank deposits, not securities.
	• SIPC — Securities Investor Protection Corporation. Up to $500,000 per customer if a BD fails, including $250,000 cash.
	• FinCEN — Financial Crimes Enforcement Network. Receives CTRs and SARs.
	• OFAC — Office of Foreign Assets Control. Sanctions; SDN list.
	• IRS — Internal Revenue Service.
	• CFTC — Commodity Futures Trading Commission. Futures and commodities.
	• ERISA — Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974. Qualified retirement plans.
	• SIPA — Securities Investor Protection Act. Created SIPC.
	• BSA — Bank Secrecy Act. AML statute.
	• USA PATRIOT Act — CIP and AML requirements for BDs.
	• SOX — Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002.
	• JOBS Act — Jumpstart Our Business Startups Act. EGC status, crowdfunding, Reg A+.
	• Reg BI — Regulation Best Interest. Retail recommendations must be in the customer’s best interest.
	• Reg T — Federal Reserve margin rule. 50% initial margin on most equity purchases.
	• Reg D — Private-offering exemption (506(b) and 506(c)).
	• Reg A / A+ — Small-issue exemption.
	• Reg S — Offshore offering exemption.
	• Reg FD — Fair Disclosure. No selective disclosure of material info.
	• Rule 144 — Resale of restricted and control stock.
	• Rule 144A — Resale to QIBs.
	• Rule 147 — Intrastate offering.
